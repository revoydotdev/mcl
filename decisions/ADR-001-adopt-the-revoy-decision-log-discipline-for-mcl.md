# ADR-001 — Adopt the revoy decision-log discipline for MCL

> **Status:** Proposed · **Implementation:** Design-only · **Reviewed:** 2026-07-07

## Context

MCL's architectural decisions to date live in prose scattered across audit files
(`final-audit.md`, `phase-*-audit.md`), roadmap preambles, and session transcripts. The
2026-07-07 Stage 3 mandate cites ADR-053 — but that ADR lives in *revoy's* decision log
(`revoy-spec/decisions/`), recording revoy's adoption of MCL's model, not MCL's own choices.
MCL is about to make a series of load-bearing, hard-to-reverse decisions (identity, parser
blessing, kernel boundary, decoding strategy); losing the "why" of any of them would repeat
the R1/R2 spec-drift failure the external postmortem documented at the language level, one
level up.

revoy already ships the machinery: standardized ADR frontmatter, `revoy adr new | list |
index`, `revoy slippage` flagging unrecorded candidate ADRs and Accepted-but-unbuilt
implementation-debt, and `revoy audit` conventions. MCL is an enrolled repo (`MCL`).

## Decision

MCL keeps its own decision log in `decisions/` at the repo root, managed exclusively through
`revoy adr`:

- every significant, durable, or surprising architectural decision gets one ADR with the
  standardized frontmatter (`adr`, `title`, `status`, `implementation`, `date`,
  `last_reviewed`, `amends`, `supersedes`, `superseded_by`, `tags`, `decision`);
- ADRs are created with `revoy adr new` (never hand-rolled frontmatter), the index in
  `decisions/README.md` is generated by `revoy adr index` and never hand-edited;
- accepted ADRs are immutable — a changed decision is a new ADR that supersedes the old one
  (`revoy adr new … --supersedes N`);
- `revoy slippage` becomes part of MCL's discipline surface: candidate ADRs named in
  ROADMAP.md must be recorded before the work that depends on them ships.

ADR-001 through ADR-012 are seeded together as **Proposed** — they are a reviewable series,
not ratified policy; each is accepted (or rejected) individually.

## Consequences

- Decision-debt is machine-visible: an Accepted ADR stuck at `Design-only` shows up in
  `revoy adr list` and `revoy slippage` instead of silently rotting.
- The roadmap docs (`docs/roadmap/`) can reference decisions by stable number instead of
  restating arguments, keeping one authoritative source per rationale.
- Slight ceremony cost per decision; mitigated by the scaffolder.

## Roads not taken

- **Keep using audit files + roadmap prose.** Rejected: those are snapshots, not decisions —
  they record what was true, not what was chosen and why, and they don't supersede cleanly.
- **Record MCL decisions in revoy-spec/decisions/.** Rejected: MCL is a standalone
  publishable project; its decision log must travel with the repo. Cross-references to
  revoy ADRs (e.g. ADR-053) remain links, not homes.
